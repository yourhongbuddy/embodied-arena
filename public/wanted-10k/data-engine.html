The daily loop

Discover → verify → design → challenge → validate → human review → version → repeat

Daily research refreshes a source ledger. The runner consumes that reviewed snapshot, produces proposals and audit records, and stops. No unreviewed proposal changes the official WANTED protocol.

100 bounded workers

10 groups × 10 assignments. Up to five model requests run concurrently by default, with 100 maximum requests per iteration and no automatic retries. Groups pass bounded, unreviewed context forward.

What we adapt—and what we do not claim

Protect the meaning of “wanted”

Measure benefit, not dependency

Participants can leave freely. Do not reward flattery, guilt, compulsive use or distress at removal. No AI-generated keep votes. Safety, privacy, and human autonomy remain gates.

Keep evidence classes separate

Real observations, human demonstrations, simulations, inferred labels and public reports are distinct. Neither 100 workers nor 10,000 simulated hours proves 10,000 hours of human coexistence.

Keep the benchmark independent

Training and sealed holdout data never mix. Group splits by environment and related source family. Never extrapolate a 10K retention score from unsupported follow-up; pooled short runs do not manufacture a long-duration result.

Collect only with permission

Evaluation, training and publication have different authorizations. Sidecar schema validation is not proof of consent or independent audit. Raw home video is not uploaded by this runner.

Developer entry point

Keep the existing signed WANTED event stream. Add purpose-limited collection sidecars and a reviewed source manifest; no new robot control interface is required.

The runner writes per-worker records, errors, usage where returned, a manifest digest and a local integrity chain. Only an independently retained root can make later edits detectable; the chain does not prove omitted events were collected.
