Version 0.1 · September 22, 2026. No platform publication or reach is claimed.

## Positioning

HILO / WANTED-10K asks whether people continue choosing to live or work with a robot over long periods. It distinguishes real resident hours, interventions, safety and privacy events, and unsupported claims. The 10,000-hour horizon is an evaluation target, not a completed study.

## X / short social draft

A robot demo lasts minutes. Living with a robot lasts months.

HILO / WANTED-10K is a benchmark proposal for voluntary retention, human intervention burden and auditable long-term coexistence.

I’m developing HILO / WANTED-10K, an independent longitudinal robot benchmark. The interesting implementation problem is distinguishing real residence time from downtime, remote assistance and synthetic data.

## Outreach pattern

Reference one verified aspect of the recipient’s public work. Explain the specific integration or methodological question, offer a runnable example, disclose the project relationship, and ask one answerable question. Keep under 140 words. No bulk BCC, repeated nonresponse sequences, invented endorsement, or hidden tracking.

## Video brief

Show a real browser session: inspect an explicitly synthetic example, reveal a deliberately omitted intervention, and show why the evidence check rejects it. End with the actual protocol link. Label the demonstration synthetic on screen; do not generate footage of a robot passing a field trial.

## Measure real outcomes

Track real public listing URLs, consented referral aggregates where available, actual installation receipts, developer integrations, replies and accepted contributions. Do not count workers, drafted copy, internal tests or self-generated visits as reach. No fake followers, stars, installs, reviews, testimonials or upvotes.
